# CS-230
SNHU CS-230 Operating Platforms coursework and projects

### Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements.

The Gaming Room was a client that wanted to expand its existing game, Draw It or Lose It, so that it could run on multiple platforms instead of only Android. The software needed to support multiple teams and players while making sure that each game, team, and player had a unique name. The client also needed a system that could support multiple users while maintaining security and performance.

### What did you do particularly well in developing this documentation?

I think one thing I did well was explaining the different operating platforms and how they would affect the application. I compared the advantages and disadvantages of different systems and considered how each one would support the client's requirements. I also focused on making the documentation organized so that the development team could understand the recommendations.

### What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?

Working through the design document helped me understand the requirements before making changes to the code. Having everything organized made it easier to identify what the application needed to accomplish. It also helped me understand how different parts of the software would work together and why certain design decisions were important.

### If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

If I could revise one part of the document, I would improve the recommendations for the different operating platforms. I would include more information about the long-term costs and maintenance requirements of each platform. This would help the client better understand how their decision could affect the application in the future.

### How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?

I approached the design by looking at what The Gaming Room needed the application to do. Since the client wanted to expand to multiple platforms, I focused on scalability, security, and making sure the application could support multiple users. Considering the user's needs is important because the software needs to solve the problems it was designed for. Without understanding those requirements, developers could create something that works but does not meet the client's expectations.

### How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?

My approach was to start with the requirements and work through the different parts of the system before deciding on a solution. I compared operating platforms, considered the application's limitations, and looked at how the design could support future development. In future projects, I would continue using design documents to organize requirements and compare possible solutions. I would also focus on testing and reviewing the design throughout development to identify problems before they become more difficult to fix.
